Expect a spread at n = 100 and n = 1000 and *no* spread at n = 9: at or below `vecBranch` (32) the keys can occupy distinct level-0 slots, so the total is already order-independent — record that as the lower bound of the defect rather than as a failure to reproduce it. Confirm the same spread appears within a single process, since Go re-randomises map iteration per range, and confirm `eachRaw`'s other Go-map walk (the `for _, e := range h.large.m` inside `func (h *HashMap) eachRaw(fn func(e entry)) {`) reaches no caller that charges **allocation** by traversal order — `boundedEquals` (`core/depth.go`) takes no budget and charges nothing, and `sortedEntries` sorts before anything reads it. ## 1. Failing contract

- [x] 1.1 Use existing-service-strict testing. Add `TestHashMap_ConversionChargeIsReproducible` in `core`: build one builder-form map above `hashMapSmallLimit`, take the conversion repeatedly against that same retained receiver, and require every reported charge to be identical; add `TestHashMap_ConversionChargeIgnoresBuildOrder`, which builds two maps of equal contents by inserting pairs in different orders and requires their conversion charges to match. Assert exact equality of charges, not a bound — reproducibility is the contract. Verify both fail at base, and report how many repeats are needed to expose the spread reliably at each size, so the committed test is not itself flaky.

## 2. Reproducible conversion

- [x] 2.1 Make the conversion's charge a function of the map's contents alone. Choose the mechanism on the evidence gathered in the design stage — a data-derived insertion order, a single order-independent build, or charging the finished structure — and record why the other two were rejected. Verify the resulting trie is unchanged in shape and contents, that `Len`, `getByHashKey`, iteration order, equality and printing do not move, and that the tests of 1.1 go green.
- [x] 2.2 Verify the charge stayed honest: the storage the conversion actually obtains is still accounted, not only what the finished trie retains, and state the measured before and after so the size of the move is on the record. Land both sets of figures as nested bullets under tasks 0.1 and 2.2 in this file, so they archive with the change.
  - Conversion now charges one number per map. Eight successive conversions charge 4400 on assoc at n=9, 4240 on dissoc at n=9, 101328 on assoc at n=100, 101200 on dissoc at n=100, 1145936 on assoc at n=1000, 1145720 on dissoc at n=1000, and 4192 for the colliding pair 3367 and 6372 at n=11 — against the earlier spreads of 66560 to 75488, 66976 to 84240, 1032528 to 1066000, 1056400 to 1066344 and 3096 to 4056. Opposite build orders now agree: 4400 against 4400 at n=9, 101328 against 101328 at n=100 and 1145936 against 1145936 at n=1000, where they were 3792 and 3792, 78384 and 80224, 1068152 and 1078672.
  - The size of the move is the entry buffer the ordering obtains plus the path-copy total that sorted insertion produces. `HashMapShallowBytes(n)` is 608 at n=9, 6432 at n=100 and 64032 at n=1000; subtracting it leaves path-copy sums of 3792, 94896 and 1081904, so at n=9 the deterministic order reproduces the old total exactly, and at the larger sizes it settles on one total that sits above the spread the map-iteration orders produced.
  - The charge still accounts for storage the conversion obtains rather than for what the finished trie keeps. Every branch of `(*hamtNode).assoc` (core/types.go:845) returns `hamtNodeBytes` of a node it has just allocated, and the descending branch returns the child's bytes plus a clone of the node at that level, so each insertion is billed a fresh copy of the entire root-to-leaf path; the next insertion supersedes those copies and the finished trie no longer holds them. The floor `retainedTrieBytes(root) + HashMapShallowBytes(n)` counts only the surviving nodes and the buffer, so the gap between the two is the discarded path copies, and it widens with the map: 100704 against a floor of 13880 at n=100, a margin of 86824, and 1144336 against 137240 at n=1000, a margin of 1007096. Both floors are unchanged from the base run, so the whole move sits in what the conversion allocates.
